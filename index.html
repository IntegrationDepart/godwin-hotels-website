Sightseeing Highlights

Typical One-Day Itinerary Highlights

Explore Delhi's most celebrated heritage landmarks, spiritual sites, and historic monuments with private chauffeured AC cabs arranged directly by our 24-hour travel desk.

Book Tour Cab

Morning Landmarks

Drive past the Rashtrapati Bhavan (President's Estate) and Parliament House, then stop at the India Gate war memorial.

Historical Wonders

Visit the towering Qutub Minar (UNESCO) and the Mughal-era architectural masterpiece Humayun's Tomb (UNESCO).

Spiritual & Cultural Sites

Explore the majestic Swaminarayan Akshardham Temple, the serene Lotus Temple, the sacred Orissan-carved Birla Mandir, and holy Bangla Sahib Gurudwara.

Old Delhi & Memorials

See Mahatma Gandhi's memorial at Raj Ghat and view the imperial Mughal Red Fort (note that the Red Fort and several museums are closed on Mondays).

Delhi Sightseeing & Airport Transfers

Our 24-hour travel desk arranges private AC cabs for Delhi city tours (Red Fort, Qutub Minar, India Gate) and Golden Triangle trips to Agra & Jaipur, plus airport pick-up with placard greeting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the most common questions about Hotel Grand Godwin and Hotel Godwin Deluxe in New Delhi.

Both hotels are on Arakashan Road, Ram Nagar, Paharganj, New Delhi 110055.
Hotel Grand Godwin is at 8502/41 and Hotel Godwin Deluxe is at 8501/15 — right next to each other, just 500 metres (5-minute walk) from New Delhi Railway Station (Ajmeri Gate exit) and 1.2 km from Connaught Place.
Hotel Grand Godwin offers classic warm hospitality and spacious family rooms; Hotel Godwin Deluxe offers sleek modern designer boutique styling.
Both are managed by the same hospitality team and share the same high standards: immaculate rooms, 24/7 security, elevator access, The Indian Grill restaurant, and rooftop terraces.
500 metres from NDLS Railway Station and 700 metres from the Airport Express Metro Station.
The New Delhi Airport Express Metro reaches Indira Gandhi International Airport (IGI) in just 22 minutes. Perfect for early flights or late arrivals.
Check-in is at 1:00 PM (1.00 Noon / 13:00); check-out is at 11:00 AM at both hotels.
Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability of the rooms. Luggage storage is always available free of charge for early arrivals or late departures.
Yes — we provide 24-hour in-house room service. You can order food in your room at any time of the day or night.
In addition to 24/7 in-house room service, guests can enjoy gourmet dining at The Indian Grill multi-cuisine restaurant at Hotel Godwin Deluxe (open daily 7:30 AM – 11:00 PM, last order taken at 10:45 PM; lavish breakfast buffet 7:30 AM – 10:30 AM) and Coffee Brownie boutique café at Hotel Grand Godwin (open daily 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM), along with open-air rooftop terraces at both hotels.
We provide an extra roll-away bed in the room whenever you require it.
For any room booked with 3 guests, we provide an extra roll-away bed in the room. It will be added whenever you require it in your room to ensure a restful and comfortable stay.
Free cancellation up to 24–48 hours before check-in, depending on your rate tier.
