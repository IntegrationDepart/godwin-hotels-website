Guest Clarifications

Hotel Godwin Deluxe FAQs

Answers to common traveler questions regarding our 26-room designer boutique hotel, air purification, and location.

How many rooms does Hotel Godwin Deluxe have?

Hotel Godwin Deluxe features exactly 26 designer boutique rooms. This boutique capacity ensures attentive service, uncrowded common areas, zero elevator waiting, and quiet relaxation.

What are the 4 room categories available?

We offer 4 distinct room categories:
• Standard ( Ground Floor ) (180 sq.ft / 17 sq.mt, Double Bed, 1 Bathroom, Max 2 Guests)
• Premier King Room with Air-Purifier (250 sq.ft / 23 sq.mt, Double Bed, 1 Bathroom, Max 3 Guests)
• Premier Twin Bed Room with Air-Purifier (288 sq.ft / 27 sq.mt, 2 x Single Bed(s), 1 Bathroom, Max 3 Guests)
• Premier Studio King Room with Air-Purifier (450 sq.ft / 42 sq.mt, Master King Bed, 1 Bathroom, Max 3 Guests)

What is the sleeping arrangement for a 3rd person in the room?

We provide an extra roll-away bed in the room. It will be added whenever you require it in your room to ensure a restful and comfortable stay.

Are air purifiers provided in all rooms?

Yes! All 26 rooms at Hotel Godwin Deluxe are equipped with high-performance HEPA air purifiers, ensuring you enjoy pure, clean, allergen-free air during your stay in Central Delhi.

How far is Hotel Godwin Deluxe from New Delhi Railway Station?

Hotel Godwin Deluxe is located at 8501/15 Arakashan Road, just 500 meters (approx. 5 minutes walk) from the Ajmeri Gate exit of New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS) and 700 meters from the Airport Express Metro Station.

What is The Indian Grill restaurant and what are its timings?

The Indian Grill is our premier multi-cuisine restaurant located right on the ground floor of Hotel Godwin Deluxe. It is open daily from 7:30 AM to 11:00 PM (last order taken at 10:45 PM). Serving gourmet North Indian Mughlai curries, clay oven kebabs, Continental, and a daily hot breakfast buffet from 7:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

What food facilities are available, and is room service 24 hours?

Yes! We have 24-hour in-house room service. You can order food in your room at any time of the day or night. Our kitchen delivers fresh, piping hot North Indian dishes, Mughlai delicacies, snacks, and beverages straight to your doorstep. You can also dine at The Indian Grill on the ground floor (7:30 AM – 11:00 PM, last order 10:45 PM) or visit Coffee Brownie cafe next door (8:00 AM – 10:00 PM).

How do I reach Hotel Godwin Deluxe from Kashmere Gate (ISBT) and Nizamuddin Station?

• From Kashmere Gate (ISBT): Approx. 5.5 km away. Take the direct Yellow Line Metro from Kashmere Gate to New Delhi Metro Station (3 stops, 7 minutes), exit towards Ajmeri Gate, and walk 5 minutes (500m) to Hotel Godwin Deluxe.

• From Hazrat Nizamuddin Railway Station (NZM): Approx. 8.5 km away (approx. 20 minutes drive). 24/7 prepaid taxis and autos are available outside the station, or call our 24/7 reception desk at +91 88600 81994 for station pickup assistance.
