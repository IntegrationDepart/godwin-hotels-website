Sightseeing Recommendation

Typical One-Day Itinerary Highlights

Our 24-hour travel desk arranges private, air-conditioned chauffeured cabs for full-day Delhi sightseeing tours departing directly from our lobby.

Book Sightseeing Tour

Morning Landmarks

Drive past the Rashtrapati Bhavan (President's Estate) and Parliament House, then stop at the India Gate war memorial.

Historical Wonders

Visit the towering Qutub Minar (world's tallest brick minaret) and the Mughal-era architectural masterpiece Humayun's Tomb.

Spiritual & Cultural Sites

Explore the majestic Swaminarayan Akshardham Temple, the serene Lotus Temple, the sacred Orissan-carved Birla Mandir, and holy Bangla Sahib Gurudwara.

Old Delhi & Memorials

See Mahatma Gandhi's memorial at Raj Ghat and view the imperial Mughal Red Fort (note that the Red Fort and several museums are closed on Mondays).

Street Food Tour

Taste iconic Delhi street food across Chandni Chowk, legendary Paranthe Wali Gali, Mughlai kebabs at Jama Masjid Matia Mahal, and spice trails at Khari Baoli.

Guided Bicycle Tour

Early morning heritage cycle rides through medieval lanes of Shahjahanabad / Old Delhi (Turkman Gate, Jama Masjid, Red Fort) and grand tree-lined boulevards of Lutyens' Delhi & Lodhi Garden.

Guest Clarifications

Hotel Grand Godwin FAQs

Answers to common traveler questions regarding our 39-room boutique hotel, location, and services.

How many rooms does Hotel Grand Godwin have?

Hotel Grand Godwin features exactly 39 boutique rooms and suites. This intimate capacity ensures uncrowded hallways, zero lift congestion, and personalized hospitality from our dedicated team.

What are the room categories available?

We offer 4 distinct room categories:
• Superior Room (180 sq.ft / 17 sq.mt, Double Bed, 1 Bathroom, Max 3 Guests)
• Executive Room (225 sq.ft / 21 sq.mt, Double Bed, 1 Bathroom, Max 3 Guests)
• Executive Twin Room (225 sq.ft / 21 sq.mt, 2 x Single Bed(s), 1 Bathroom, Max 3 Guests)
• Studio Room with Air-Purifier (275 sq.ft / 26 sq.mt, City View, Double Bed, 1 Bathroom, Max 3 Guests)

What is the sleeping arrangement for a 3rd person in the room?

We provide an extra roll-away bed in the room. It will be added whenever you require it in your room to ensure a restful and comfortable stay.

How far is the hotel from New Delhi Railway Station?

Hotel Grand Godwin is just 500 meters (approx. 5 minutes walk) from the Ajmeri Gate exit of New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS) and 700 meters from the Airport Express Metro Station.

What are the timings for Coffee Brownie cafe?

Coffee Brownie cafe is open daily from 8:00 AM in the morning to 10:00 PM at night, serving fresh artisanal brews, espresso, teas, sandwiches, warm brownies, and pastries.

What food facilities are available, and is room service 24 hours?

Yes! We have 24-hour in-house room service. You can order fresh, delicious meals, snacks, and beverages directly to your room at any time of the day or night. Guests also enjoy dining at The Indian Grill restaurant (open daily 7:30 AM to 11:00 PM, last order taken at 10:45 PM; daily buffet breakfast 7:30 AM to 10:30 AM) and Coffee Brownie boutique cafe (open daily 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM).

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Standard check-in is at 1:00 PM (1.00 Noon / 13:00) and check-out is at 11:00 AM (11:00). Both early check-in and late check-out are strictly subject to availability of the rooms upon request. 24-hour secure luggage storage is always complimentary for early arrivals or late departures.

How do I reach the hotel from Kashmere Gate (ISBT) and Nizamuddin Railway Station?

• From Kashmere Gate (ISBT): Located approx. 5.5 km away. By Delhi Metro, take the direct Yellow Line from Kashmere Gate Station to New Delhi Metro Station (just 3 stops, 7 minutes), take the Ajmeri Gate exit, and walk 5 minutes (500m) to the hotel. By auto/cab, travel time is approx. 15 minutes.

• From Hazrat Nizamuddin Railway Station (NZM): Located approx. 8.5 km away (approx. 20–25 minutes drive via Mathura Road / Barakhamba). Direct prepaid cabs and auto-rickshaws are available 24/7 at the station, or contact our front desk at +91 88600 81994 to arrange chauffeured station pick-up.
